Observatory

A night-time reading room in an old observatory. The page is deep navy, nearly flat, with a satin sheen you only notice when you tilt your head. The instruments are drawn in hairline silver-gilt: an astrolabe’s rete, the rings of an orrery, graduated scales and a vernier. Real brass appears once on each screen, on the one thing to look at or touch. It is the quietest of the five directions, and the cheapest to draw.

Borrows from Leather & Ink: the type pairing’s display face (Cormorant Garamond), italic labels in place of capitals, the emboss and deboss vocabulary (made crisper, like engraved plates), one framed object per page, and gold kept for ornament. It drops the grain, the stitching and the rose.
