Star Atlas

A plate from an eighteenth-century celestial atlas, printed in gold on Prussian-blue laid paper. Fine linework does the work leather and stitching did: a faint graticule behind every page, graduated scales for dividers, stars drawn by magnitude. Your reading becomes a constellation, lit chapter by chapter. Scholarly and a little romantic; the ornament is precise rather than lavish.

Borrows from Leather & Ink: the role split (gold for structure, one warm accent for interaction, now cinnabar instead of rose), italic labels and running heads, the emboss and press recipes, the one-memorable-object rule, and the soft-ink drawing manner. Leaves behind leather, stitching and tape.

Details up close

Each sample notes what it costs to draw in SwiftUI, for the drawing-cost pass running alongside.

Laid paper, Prussian blue

Chain lines every 128pt, laid lines every 3, speckle and fibre. Lamp from the top left; vignette below.

cheap Bake the laid lines, speckle and fibre into one 256px tile (like Grain.tile) and tile it; the lamp and vignette are two static gradients. Never run the turbulence live.

The graticule

Declination circles and right-ascension meridians from a pole off the top left, with a dotted ecliptic.

cheap One Path in a Canvas behind the scroll view, or a vector PDF in the asset catalog. Pin it to the window, not to rows, so it never redraws on scroll. Reader drops it to 12%.

Gold: printed and foil-stamped

Left, flat printed gold, as an engraved plate would be. Right, foil: a five-stop gradient, a 1px dark shadow below and a faint light edge above.

LyraLyra
cheap printed: a plain foregroundStyle(gold). moderate foil: a LinearGradient style plus two small shadows. Fine for a title or the icon; not for every row title.

Star motifs

Magnitude stars from an atlas legend, a constellation lettered in spaced capitals, and a fragment of an astrolabe rete with its ecliptic ring in cinnabar.

cheap Each magnitude is one small vector image or custom SF Symbol, so it scales with Dynamic Type. Constellations and the rete are static Paths. Cinzel only for these figure names: the one place the theme allows spaced capitals, because that is how an atlas letters a constellation.

Buttons

Primary: a raised plate with an inner gold hairline and a cinnabar italic label. Secondary: raised, cream ink. Pressed: printed into the paper.

Dividers and ornaments

The graduated scale heads each group: ticks every 6, long ticks every 30, like an atlas margin. The Pleiades heads a chapter. Plate frames take the double rule.

cheap The scale is a dashed StrokeStyle (two dash patterns) on one line. Use it for group heads only; row separators stay plain hairlines, so a long list draws no ticks.

Progress as a constellation

The signature. Every chapter is a star sized by its length. Read chapters are lit, the current one ringed in cinnabar, unread ones open, unposted ones dotted.

cheap One Canvas with resolved star symbols: a few dozen draws, redrawn only when progress changes. Lay out a long fic (40+ chapters) on a wandering path that wraps into rows; past about 60 stars, group by tens.
