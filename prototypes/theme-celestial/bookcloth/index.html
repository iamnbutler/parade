Direction one

Midnight Bookcloth

The same book as Leather & Ink, rebound. Deep indigo linen with a visible weave, panels pressed blind into the cloth, and gold that is tooled, not printed: double fillets, small eight-point stars at the corners, a title stamped in foil. It is a binder's idea of the night sky, a few stars and a lot of quiet blue, so the reading still comes first.

Borrows from Leather & Ink: both typefaces, the italic gold labels, emboss and deboss, the one signature object per page (the stitched card becomes a gilt-tooled panel), dusty rose for interaction, the grain tile and the soft-ink drawings. What changes is the material, cloth instead of leather, and the ornament, tooling and stars instead of stitching.

Up close

Each effect with a note on what it costs to draw in SwiftUI, for the perf pass. The rule of thumb: bake textures once into a tile, keep gradients static, and never blur or blend per row.

Indigo linen

Warp and weft at a 3 px pitch, a darker under-thread, long uneven slubs, fine grain, lit from the top left with a vignette below. On the right, the weave at 4×.

moderate, once Build it as one baked tile (a 256 px CGImage like Grain.tile, with the weave, slubs and grain together) drawn with .resizable(resizingMode: .tile) over two static gradients. Never draw the weave as stripes in SwiftUI: hundreds of rectangles per frame. Cost after launch is one textured quad.

Gold foil, stamped

A warm gold with a bright band where the lamp catches it, speckled like real foil, with a dark lip above (pressed into the cloth) and a faint light lip below.

cheap A static LinearGradient as the text's foregroundStyle, plus two 0-radius offset shadows for the lips. moderate The speckle needs the gradient and noise flattened into one image used as the fill; keep it to titles and the one primary button, never list rows.
