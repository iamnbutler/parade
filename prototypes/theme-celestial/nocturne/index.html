Nocturne

A keepsake book bound in sapphire velvet, the kind you keep on the nightstand. The nap takes the light along one diagonal, a gold silk ribbon hangs from the top of the page you’re on, and a crescent is foil-stamped where Leather & Ink stitched its card. The motif carries meaning: the moon waxes as you read, new on a fic you haven’t opened, full on one you’ve finished, and every chapter is a phase. Stars are a dusting, never a starfield. The most romantic of the five.

Borrows from Leather & Ink: the emboss and deboss recipes (re-lit in cool blue), the inset hairline on the one signature card, italic display labels in gold, the drop-cap reader, the one-accent rule (blush plays rose’s part), the soft-ink drawing style.

Up close

Every effect, with what it would cost to draw in SwiftUI. The rule of thumb: anything static and drawn once is cheap; anything blended, blurred or masked per row, or that moves with the device, is not.

Sapphire velvet

Four layers: the base color, a diagonal sheen band at 122° (the nap catching the lamp), a slow crushed-velvet mottle, and a fine nap grain. The reader keeps only a trace of each.

The page: sheen 9%, crush 14%, nap 30%

The moon, and what it means

Reading progress as a phase. A new moon is unread, waxing is under way, full is finished. Each chapter in a list is drawn at its place in the fic.

What each effect costs in SwiftUI

For the drawing-cost pass. “Cheap” means drawn once and cached by the render server; “costly” means work every frame or per row.

EffectCostHow to build it cheaply
Velvet base and lampcheapStatic color plus two EllipticalGradients in the existing LeatherBackground; one view behind the whole window (the leatherBackdrop trick on Mac).
Diagonal sheencheapOne LinearGradient with five stops, like Noir’s silk. Don’t tie it to CMMotionManager; if wanted at all, only on the icon via the system’s own glass.
Nap and crush texturecheapBake both into one CGImage tile at launch (as Grain.tile does), tint pre-applied, draw with resizingMode: .tile and normal blending. The CSS here uses overlay and soft-light; SwiftUI shouldn’t.
Dust starscheapA baked tile, masked by a gradient once at the top of the list. The fade can be a gradient in the tile itself, avoiding .mask.
Foil textmoderateGradient foregroundStyle and two 1-pt shadows on titles only. Flat #d6b46e for row-level gold.
Embossed / debossed cards and chipscheapThe existing Embossed and Debossed modifiers with new colors. Avoid a 15-pt blurred .shadow on every row; only the summary card is lifted.
Moon phasescheapA Shape taking illumination: Double: an arc plus an elliptical arc. Use .drawingGroup() only for the 14-moon chapter strip on Mac.
RibboncheapA custom Shape, two gradients, one shadow, placed in an overlay that doesn’t scroll.
Glass tab bar and toolbarscheapNative iOS 26 glass with a sapphire .tint; the system already pays for the blur. Don’t add a custom .background(.ultraThinMaterial) on top.
Empty-state drawingcheapA template PDF or SVG asset tinted with the palette, as the current drawings are.
App icon embosscheapBaked in the icon; in Icon Composer, three layers and the system’s specular.
