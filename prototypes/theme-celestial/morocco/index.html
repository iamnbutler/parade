Blue Morocco

Leather & Ink rebound in deep navy goatskin. The grain is a fine, even pebble you feel more than see, the light comes from a lamp at the top left, and the gold is tooled: a blind line and a gilt line together, a star tool used sparingly, gold saddle stitching on the one card each page gets. The sky arrives in one place, where it means something: a fic's chapters are drawn as a constellation, and you read your way across it.

Borrows from Leather & Ink: almost all of it. Both typefaces, the leather itself (pebbled instead of plain), the lamp and vignette, emboss and deboss, the stitched card (now gold thread), italic gold labels, dusty rose for interaction, and the soft-ink drawings. What's new is the color and the chapter constellation.

Changed after round one: no directional texture anywhere (the grain is isotropic and baked once, at very low contrast; the reader is nearly smooth). Every control row uses one button component at one height on a shared column grid; the primary differs only in fill and foil.

The app icon

Today's icon, rebound: the same embossed Cormorant P and stitched border, on blue pebbled goatskin. One gilt star tool sits beside the bowl of the P.

Same recipe as docs/app-icon.md (pebble, lamp, worn edge, grooved stitching, specular P), with the leather changed to navy and the thread a touch brighter so it holds against blue. At 60 points the stitching still reads as a row of stitches and the star as a glint.

Up close

Each piece with what it would cost to draw in SwiftUI. The short version: one baked tile, static gradients and strokes; nothing blends or blurs while you scroll.

Morocco grain

An even pebble, lit from the top left, at very low contrast. Left at 100%; right magnified four times, with its contrast raised so you can see the pebble.

cheap One tile, baked once. Render the relief and the soft-light blend into a 200 pt CGImage at launch, exactly like Grain.tile, and tile it with resizingMode: .tile. Don't use .blendMode(.softLight) live: it forces an offscreen pass on every scroll frame.
