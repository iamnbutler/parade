Midnight Calf

Parade bound in polished blue calfskin, the deepest navy a binder would dye, on a book that has been carried and opened for years. There is almost no grain: the leather is smooth, and what you feel is a burnished sheen from one lamp at the top left and a soft darkening toward the edges. Gold is crisp and stamped: fillets with a lit edge, a turn-in roll framing the one card on each page, and a small crescent-and-star tool for a fleuron. The moon carries meaning: it waxes as you read, new on a fic you haven't opened, full on one you've finished.

Borrows from Leather & Ink: Cormorant Garamond and Newsreader, the emboss and deboss recipes (re-lit cool), one signature card per page, gold for ornament and rose for interaction, italic labels, the soft-ink drawings. It drops the pebbled grain and the stitching. From round one it keeps Nocturne's moon-phase progress and Bookcloth's gilt fillets.

Cormorant Garamond, display

“Six you can see. Nine if you've got good eyes and no streetlights.” Newsreader carries the reading at 18 / 30 in the reader.

Why not EB Garamond for body: its hairlines thin out on a ground this dark, and Newsreader's sturdier strokes and taller x-height hold up at 15–18px. The app already ships both faces.
