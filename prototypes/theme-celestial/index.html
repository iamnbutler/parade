Parade · a new binding

Five ways to bind Parade in blue and gold

Each direction keeps Parade's native structure (tab bar, split view, toolbars, the custom rows and cards) and keeps Leather & Ink's rules: one dark theme, tactile but quiet, literary type, drawings rather than icons. What changes is the binding. Every page shows the same fics on the same screens: library, a fic page, the reader, an empty state, the chrome close up, the app icon, the details, and a Mac window.

The recommendation

Midnight Bookcloth as the base, with Star Atlas's chapter constellation as its one signature object.

Bookcloth is the brief's own image (a blue cloth book, gold-tooled), and it is the cheapest move from today's code: same fonts, same emboss, deboss and card recipes, a new palette and one baked weave tile. On its own it's close to a recolour, though. The Atlas's chapter chart (read chapters lit, the current one ringed, unposted ones dotted) gives it a celestial idea that means something, and it is one Canvas drawn once per fic page.

Runners-up: Nocturne's moon-phase progress is the most romantic idea here, but Playfair italic gets dressy fast. Observatory is the quietest and cheapest, but at a glance it reads as a nice dark app more than a bound book.

Star Atlas

Type
IM Fell English for display, EB Garamond for body, Cinzel capitals only where an atlas letters a constellation (a deliberate, contained exception).
Signature
Chapters as a constellation: read stars lit, the current one ringed in cinnabar, unread open, unposted dotted.
Borrows
The rose's job goes to cinnabar (the atlas's ecliptic red); gold stays structural; italic labels.
Cost
Moderate. The paper is a baked tile; the graticule and chart are Paths drawn once, never on scroll.
