Parade · a new binding

Binding Parade in blue and gold

Each direction keeps Parade's native structure (tab bar, split view, toolbars, the custom rows and cards) and keeps Leather & Ink's rules: one dark theme, tactile but quiet, literary type, drawings rather than icons. What changes is the binding. Every page shows the same fics on the same screens: library, a fic page, the reader, an empty state, the chrome close up, the app icon, the details, and a Mac window.

The recommendation, after round two

Blue Morocco: Leather & Ink in deep navy goatskin, with the chapter constellation as its one celestial idea.

Round one's grounds were too busy, and the linen weave read as horizontal grain. Round two goes back to leather in a deeper blue, with a texture you feel rather than see. Morocco is the closest to today's app: the same fonts, stitched card, emboss and deboss, and LeatherBackground with a new palette and one baked pebble tile, so it's also the cheapest port.

Midnight Calf is the calmer alternative: smooth burnished calf with almost no grain, a gilt turn-in border, and moon phases for reading progress. Round one stays below for its ideas.

Round two: deep blue leather

Quieter grounds with no directional texture, and one button component per row: the same height and baseline, the primary set apart by fill alone.
